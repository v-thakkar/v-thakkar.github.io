# About

I'm Vaishali, a Linux kernel and virtualization engineer based in Berlin,
Germany.

I've spent the last decade working on systems software, mostly in the Linux
kernel, and these days I focus on confidential computing. I also occasionally
work on paid and volunteer projects around open source community management and
technical writing.

In the past, I volunteered with [Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/) as a
coordinator for Linux kernel projects and co-organized
[Rails Girls Summer of Code](https://railsgirlssummerofcode.org/) with the
Travis Foundation.

I enjoy sharing knowledge about software development and systems programming.
Earlier in my career, I ran Linux kernel workshops at Indian universities; the
[workshop material](https://github.com/v-thakkar/Linux-Kernel-Workshop) and
[slides from my talks](https://github.com/v-thakkar/Talks) are available here.

If you'd like to discuss any of the above, feel free to email me at
`me dot vaishalithakkar at gmail dot com`. I'm generally happy to connect with
like-minded people.
