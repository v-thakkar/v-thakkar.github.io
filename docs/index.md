# About

I'm Vaishali, a Linux kernel and virtualization engineer based in Berlin,
Germany. I have more than a decade of experience building systems software,
with work spanning kernel development, virtualization, eBPF, and confidential
computing.

## Technical leadership

I bring more than ten years of experience in Linux kernel and systems software
and currently lead confidential computing efforts at SUSE. I also occassionally serve
on the technical program committees for the conferences like
[eBPF Summit](https://ebpf.io/summit-2022.html) and the
[Open Confidential Computing Conference (OC3)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/edgeless-systems_program-committee-oc3-2026-activity-7392486013480218624-RcsR?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAABNjjY0BYW1K8S_HKRX4FU9vT9RWSK_cXuk).

## Technical speaking

I have spoken at international open source and systems conferences,
including KVM Forum, FOSDEM, Linux Plumbers Conference, Kernel Recipes, and
LinuxCon. You can find a selection on my [talks page](talks.md).

## Mentorship and community building

Over the years, I have helped many aspiring contributors navigate kernel
development. I also ran Linux kernel workshops at universities across India;
the [workshop material](https://github.com/v-thakkar/Linux-Kernel-Workshop)
remains publicly available. In the past, I organized Linux kernel and Rust
meetups in Bangalore, as well as the Kernel BoF at DevConf.in.

I'm also passionate about increasing diversity in open source coommunities.
I have served as a Linux kernel coordinator for
[Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org/) and co-organized
[Rails Girls Summer of Code](https://railsgirlssummerofcode.org/).
